About EDC

Where engineering curiosity becomes entrepreneurial action.

EDC HITAM helps students move from classroom ideas to practical, confident experimentation through mentorship, events, exposure visits, and a community that takes initiative seriously.

We nurture the startup mindset at the grassroots level, helping students identify problems, shape solutions, understand customers, and build the confidence to present their ideas.

The cell acts as a bridge between innovators, mentors, alumni, industry guests, and institutional support so that every student has a clearer path from spark to prototype.

About EDC HITAM

The Entrepreneurship Development Cell (EDC) was established at HITAM in 2018. It operates under the HITAM Innovation and Incubation Centre (HIIC), the official incubator of HITAM, located on the first floor of the Activity Block.

EDC focuses on developing an entrepreneurial mindset among student bodies, encouraging them to design outside bounds, and validating raw engineering prototypes into viable commercial startups through customized mentorship models.

🎯 Vision

To build an inclusive entrepreneurial ecosystem that empowers students to innovate fearlessly, develop sustainable ventures and emerge as socially responsible leaders driving global impact.

🚀 Mission & Strategic Goals

  • Skill Development: Provide opportunities for experiential learning through industry hackathons.
  • Incubation Pipelines: Facilitate the creation of at least 5 student-led startups annually inside HIIC boundaries.
  • Corporate Synergy: Build active joint venture relationships with tech networks and startup accelerators.
  • Sustainable Impact: Encourage students to deploy eco-responsible methodologies inside campus frameworks.

Students will gain knowledge of market opportunities, funding, financial planning, and legal compliance.

Students will build professional connections with mentors, industry experts, and investors through events.
